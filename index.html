How we work together

You keep your cloud. We run the control plane.

Strata never takes over your cloud account. Your infrastructure runs entirely inside AWS, GCP, or Azure — under your billing, your compliance boundary, your control. What we own is the control plane: the provisioning engine, the state files, and the scaling policies that keep it resilient.

Yours

Your cloud

  • Compute, storage & network
  • Billed directly by your provider
  • Stays inside your account & compliance boundary
provisions & orchestrates

Ours

Strata control plane

  • Provisioning engine
  • Terraform / OpenTofu state files
  • Scaling policies & load-test engine

You pay your cloud provider directly for what you use. You pay Strata a platform fee to own, orchestrate, and continuously prove the resilience of it.
