In your account

You keep your cloud. We run the control plane.

The account stays yours. Infra runs inside AWS, GCP, or Azure, on your bill. We never take the account. You grant a role only after you approve a plan.

You pay your cloud provider for what you use. You pay Strata to stand it up and load-test it.

The role we need

A scoped role that can create and manage the services in the approved plan. We do not become the account owner. We do not need your application data or your users’ data.

What “we infer” means

If package.json depends on pg, the plan says PostgreSQL and cites the file that caused it. Nothing is created until you approve that plan and connect the account.

If you leave

The infra stays in your account, on your bill. You can keep running it without us. We do not hold your data hostage.

What we read today. JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python. Other languages get a thin plan rather than a guessed one.

Where it lands. Your AWS, GCP, or Azure account — you tell us which. Connecting the cloud is a later step, after the plan.
