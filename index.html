FAQ

Questions teams ask before they send a repo.

Something else? Book a 20-minute review and ask us directly.

What is Alineplane?

Alineplane turns your GitHub repo into audit-ready production infrastructure. You review an evidence-backed plan with Crossplane claims, then we onboard and apply it with you, keeping desired, actual, and proof in sync.

How do you decide what infrastructure I need?

We infer it from your code and cite the file that caused it. If package.json depends on pg, the plan says PostgreSQL and points to that line. If we can’t provision something yet, the plan says so instead of guessing.

Which languages do you support?

JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python today. Other languages get a thin plan rather than a guessed one.

Will anything be created without my approval?

No. Nothing is created until you approve the plan.

What access do you need to my cloud account?

A scoped role that can create and manage only the services in your approved plan, granted after you approve. Access uses a federated trust chain (AWS role assumption, Azure managed identity, GCP service-account impersonation) — we never create or store long-lived static credentials for your account.

Do you access my application or user data?

No. We don’t need your application data or your users’ data.

Are my environments kept separate from other customers?

Yes. Each client is onboarded with its own provider configuration, scoped to its own cloud account.

Which clouds do you support?

Azure first; AWS and GCP in preview. Everything runs in your own account, on your bill.

Is cloud spend included in the price?

No. You pay your cloud provider directly for what you use, and you can move between tiers at any time without throwing away your plan.

Will Alineplane make me SOC 2 / HIPAA / PCI compliant?

We provide enforced infrastructure controls, a change trail, control mappings, and — on Standard and Enterprise — packaged evidence. Your auditor certifies you; we never promise a certification date.

What happens if I leave?

Your infrastructure stays in your account, on your bill, and you can keep running it without us.
