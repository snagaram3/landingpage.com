FAQ

Questions teams ask before they paste a repo.

Getting started

What is Strata?

Strata turns your GitHub repo into audit-ready production infrastructure. Paste a repo, review an evidence-backed plan with Crossplane claims, then we onboard and apply with you, keeping desired, actual and proof in sync.

Who is Strata for?

Post-traction teams with 1,000+ monthly active users, especially if a customer, investor or buyer has asked whether you're SOC 2 ready. If you're pre-launch or still finding product-market fit, a managed host like Vercel, Railway or Render is a better fit for now.

Do I need to sign up to see a plan?

No. Paste a public GitHub repo and you'll get a plan with no signup. You can also book a 20-minute infrastructure review.

Which languages do you support?

Today we read JavaScript, TypeScript and Python. Other languages get a thin plan rather than a guessed one.

How it works

What happens after I paste my repo?

Four steps: paste the repo, review the plan (evidence, diagram, cost), onboard and apply the infrastructure with you, then watch live resources and load proof on your dashboard.

How do you decide what infrastructure I need?

We infer it from your code and cite the file that caused it. For example, if package.json depends on pg, the plan says PostgreSQL and points to that file. If we can't provision something yet, the plan says so.

Will anything be created without my approval?

No. Nothing is created until you approve the plan.

Security and access

What access do you need to my cloud account?

A scoped role that can create and manage the services in your approved plan, granted only after you approve. We don't become the account owner. Under the hood, access uses a federated trust chain (AWS role assumption, Azure managed identity, GCP service-account impersonation); we don't create or store long-lived static credentials for your account.

Do you access my application or user data?

No. We don't need your application data or your users' data.

Are my environments kept separate from other customers?

Yes. Each client is onboarded with its own provider configuration and credentials scoped to its own cloud account.

Clouds supported

Which clouds do you support?

Azure first; AWS and GCP in preview. Your infrastructure runs in your own account, on your bill. You tell us which cloud; connecting it is a step after the plan.

Pricing and tiers

How much does Strata cost?

Lightweight starts at $500/month (1K–10K MAU), Standard at $2,500/month (10K–100K MAU), and Enterprise is custom, starting at $10K+/month (100K+ MAU or strict mandates).

Is cloud spend included?

No. You pay your cloud provider directly for what you use. You pay Strata to plan, stand it up and keep it in sync.

Can I change tiers later?

Yes. You can move between tiers without throwing away your plan.

Support and scaling

What uptime and support do I get?

Lightweight: 99.5% best effort. Standard: 99.9% with incident response. Enterprise: 99.95% with 24/7 support and DR runbooks.

Will Strata make me SOC 2 / HIPAA / PCI compliant?

We provide enforced infrastructure controls (encryption at rest, private networking, audit logging at creation), a change trail, control mappings, and — on Standard and Enterprise — packaged evidence. Your auditor certifies you; we never promise a certification date.

Data and offboarding

What happens if I leave?

Your infrastructure stays in your account, on your bill, and you can keep running it without us. We don't hold your data hostage.
